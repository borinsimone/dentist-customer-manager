# Resend Pricing (as provided)

## Free Plan

- $0/month
- 3,000 emails/month
- 100 emails per day limit
- 1-day data retention
- 1 domain

## Pro Plan

- $20/month
- 50,000 emails/month
- Extra emails: $0.90/1,000
- No daily limit
- 3-day data retention
- 10 domains

## Scale Plan

- $90/month
- 100,000 emails/month
- Extra emails: $0.90/1,000
- No daily limit
- 7-day data retention
- 1,000 domains

## Enterprise Plan

- Custom pricing
- Based on specific needs
- Flexible data retention and domains
- No daily limit

## Pay-as-you-go Pricing

- Teams with paid transactional plans can enable pay-as-you-go pricing for overages.
- When enabled, Resend automatically charges the overage price per each bucket of 1,000 additional emails.
- Additional emails are billed in buckets of 1,000 emails.

## Add-ons

- Dedicated IPs: $30/month for customers on the Scale plan exceeding 500 emails sent per day.

Source: https://resend.com/pricing
